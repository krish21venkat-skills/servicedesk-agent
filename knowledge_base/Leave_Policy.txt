Company Leave Policy 
1. Purpose 
This document outlines the leave entitlements and procedures for employees. It 
ensures consistency, fairness, and compliance with company standards. 
2. Types of Leave 
• Casual Leave (CL): 
o 12 days per calendar year. 
o Can be taken for personal reasons or emergencies. 
• Sick Leave (SL): 
o 10 days per calendar year. 
o Medical certificate required for absences longer than 2 days. 
• Earned Leave (EL): 
o 15 days per calendar year. 
o Must be applied at least 7 days in advance. 
• Maternity Leave: 
o 26 weeks as per statutory guidelines. 
• Paternity Leave: 
o 10 days within 6 months of child’s birth. 
3. Leave Application Process 
• Employees must apply for leave via the HR portal or email to their manager. 
• Emergency leave should be notified within 24 hours. 
• Approval is subject to workload and manager discretion. 
4. Carry Forward & Encashment 
• Unused Earned Leave can be carried forward up to 30 days. 
• Encashment of Earned Leave is allowed at the end of the financial year. 
• Casual and Sick Leave cannot be carried forward. 
5. General Guidelines 
• Unauthorized absence may lead to disciplinary action. 
• Public holidays are separate from leave entitlements. 
• HR reserves the right to amend this policy with prior notice.
